Philips Ambilight × Govee

Extend your Ambilight into the whole room.

AmbiGoveeTV synchronizes compatible Govee LAN lights with compatible Philips Ambilight Android TV / Google TV models. No camera, no HDMI sync box, no Home Assistant and no PC that must stay on.

Download AmbiGoveeTV View on GitHub Français

Can Govee lights sync with Philips Ambilight?

Yes, when both devices expose the local capabilities AmbiGoveeTV needs. Philips provides the Ambilight colors; AmbiGoveeTV forwards them to Govee over the LAN.

Do I need a camera?

No. The app uses the Ambilight data calculated by the TV itself.

Do I need a PC running while I watch TV?

No. The desktop installer is only for installation. The Android TV app runs on the TV afterwards.

AmbiGoveeTV is an independent open-source project and is not affiliated with Philips or Govee.
